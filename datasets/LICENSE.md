# Licence of our annotation files

PROPOSED, NOT FINAL: pending the licence decision for the release. Replace this file before publishing.

Proposed: our annotation files (`shadow_v1/annotations.json`, `hard_mined_v2/annotations.json`, and the `images.jsonl`
source lists) under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The images are not covered by this licence. Each image keeps the licence of its source, which `images.jsonl` lists per
image (HaGRID public licence, CC BY 2.0, CC BY-SA 2.0 / 4.0, CC0). The bench2d_v2 and low-light labels are the
upstream datasets' and follow their terms.
